From this frame:

    What counts (the challenge's definitions)
    • While the ball is in play, one team always has it. A loose or contested ball stays with the last team that had it.
    • Possession changes only when the other team gains controlled possession: it keeps the ball, or strings passes together.
    • Not a change: a touch without control, a deflection that does not intercept, an aerial duel won with no control after it.
    • Dead ball from the moment play stops until the restart is taken. Do not say who owns a dead ball.
    • Mark the change at the first frame of the new team's control.
